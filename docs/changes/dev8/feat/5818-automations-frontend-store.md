### Changed

- Macros and workflows are now saved in one automation library (#5818). The Macros and
  Workflows sidebars look and work as before; your existing macros and workflows were
  moved over automatically. An older termiHub still sees the library as it was before the
  move, and changes it makes there are merged back the next time you start this version,
  with a notice saying how many.
- A macro or workflow you deleted in an earlier development build stays deleted after the
  move (#5971).
- A macro now plays at its saved typing speed from every place you start it; the Play
  dialog's speed choice applies to that one playback only.
