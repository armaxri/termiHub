### Changed

- The terminal toolbar has one "+", and it opens a new terminal. With a single
  tab group no "Main" pill shows any more; the tab-group control reads
  "New tab group" with a stack icon, and becomes an icon after the chips once
  there are several groups.
- Split right and split down use split-square icons that no longer look like
  the sidebar toggle, and the sidebar toggle sits behind a divider. It shows
  as pressed while the sidebar is open.
- In the terminal's "…" menu, "Log session output" and "Record macro" are
  ticked while they run, instead of changing their wording.
