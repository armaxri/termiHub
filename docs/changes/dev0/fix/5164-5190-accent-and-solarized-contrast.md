### Fixed

- Accessibility: white text on primary buttons now meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) in the Dark
  and both Solarized themes. Their accent blue is slightly darker (same hue), and the
  button hover state now darkens instead of lightening (#5164).
- Accessibility: Solarized Dark and Solarized Light text now meets WCAG AA. Primary,
  secondary and muted text use stronger tones from the canonical Solarized palette, so
  both themes keep their look while labels on the sidebar and on selected rows stay
  readable (#5190).
