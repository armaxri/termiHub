### Changed

- The Settings categories sit under headings (General, Terminal, Connections,
  Security and data). Rarely used pages (Plugins, Import & migration,
  Schedules, Portable mode, X server) fold under Advanced, which opens on click
  or when you search for one of its settings (#5561).
- Settings field labels are in sentence case instead of ALL CAPS, and the
  "Allow experimental features" toggle follows suit (#5561).
- Settings no longer repeat the version and commit at the bottom of every page.
  Settings ▸ Updates shows them once, with a button that copies both (#5561).
- Appearance shows Edit, Export and Delete only while a custom theme is
  selected. Font family stays empty at its default, with a "Default" marker,
  a Reset button for a custom value and the default fonts listed in its hint
  (#5561).
- Keyboard shortcuts offer "Reset to default" only on rows you changed,
  clearing a shortcut shows a toast with Undo, and the two help rows read
  "Show keyboard shortcuts" and "Show keyboard shortcuts outside terminals"
  (#5561).
- The "Command marks by default" setting explains in plain words what it does
  (#5561).
