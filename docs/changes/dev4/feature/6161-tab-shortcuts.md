### Added

- Tabs: switch to tab 1–9 with Cmd+1…9 on macOS and Alt+Shift+1…9 on Windows and Linux (9 is the
  last tab). These keys never reach a terminal program.
- Tabs: Reopen closed tab (Ctrl+Cmd+T / Alt+Shift+T) brings back the last closed terminal tab,
  also one closed with its panel or tab group, without re-running its startup command.
- Tabs: Close others and Close tabs to the right in the tab menu, with the usual close
  confirmation, and a double-click on a tab renames it. (#6161)
