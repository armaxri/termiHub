1 · Transfer queue — multiple active (expanded)

The connection-type-agnostic transfer queue A docks directly above the status-bar. Each row shows direction, name, remote path, a progress bar, percent, throughput, and per-transfer controls. The queue covers the full state space: active B, paused C, queued D, completed E, and failed/retry F.

  • A Transfer queue panel — docks above the status bar, shared across all transfer-capable sessions.
  • B Active transfer — accent progress bar, live percent + throughput, Pause / Cancel.
  • C Paused transfer — amber bar (--state-connecting), Resume / Cancel.
  • D Queued transfer — waiting for a free slot (max 2 concurrent), clock badge, Cancel.
  • E Completed transfer — green bar (--state-connected), stays until cleared.
  • F Failed transfer — red bar (--state-disconnected), retry counter, Retry / Cancel.
